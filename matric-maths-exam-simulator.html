How it works

1

Learn the topic. Check the syllabus page — every Class 9–10 topic family, the board chapters it covers, and what you must be able to do.

2

Practise the exercises. 25 short exercises, one idea at a time, each with its answer underneath. Do them without looking first.

3

Sit the paper. Pick a paper and start the 3-hour clock. Section A multiple-choice is marked automatically as soon as you submit.

4

Mark B & C yourself. Work Sections B and C on paper, then compare with the model answers and award your marks honestly — exactly how the pack's answer key works.

The bands: 40+ is excellent, 30–39 is good, below 30 means revise that paper's topics and try again. Paper 5 is a mixed revision paper — save it for last, a week before the exam.
