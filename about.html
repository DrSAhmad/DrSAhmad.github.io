About

Dr. S. Ahmad is a psychologist, educationist, Islamic scholar and author based in Pakistan. His work sits at the meeting point of modern psychology, Islamic wisdom and practical everyday tools.

What you'll find here

This hub connects all of his free public projects:

  • Islam — a complete Islamic companion app (Quran, prayer times, Qibla, hadith, fiqh, history)
  • Zubaan — a 14-language learning platform with audio and practice modes
  • PakZameen — a property portal for Pakistan's buyers, sellers and agencies
  • Books & mentoring — writings on decisions, speech and relationships, plus publishing services
  • News site — daily headlines and free health planners
  • Milo's World Videos — educational cartoons for children

Philosophy

Knowledge should be free and useful. Every project here is built to benefit people directly — no paywalls on learning, faith or children's content.
