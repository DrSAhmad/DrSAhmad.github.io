🏔️ Hunza Valley — Five Days in Pakistan's North

Mountains, lakes and forts. Plan the journey, enjoy the moment.

5 days / 4 nights Leisure & Exploration Friends / Family / Couple Budget: $500 per person (estimated, on-ground)

Highlights

  • Baltit Fort & Altit Fort, Karimabad
  • Attabad Lake, Passu Cones & Hussaini Suspension Bridge
  • Eagle's Nest sunset point & Karimabad bazaar
  • Gulmit village, apricot & cherry orchards in season
  • Extension idea: Skardu next — Shigar Fort, Khaplu Palace, Deosai Plains, Satpara & Shangrila Lakes

The five days

  • Day 1 — Arrival: reach Karimabad via Gilgit, settle in, gentle bazaar walk.
  • Day 2 — Discover: Baltit Fort in the morning, Altit Fort after lunch, Eagle's Nest at sunset.
  • Day 3 — Culture & Lakes: Attabad Lake boating, Passu Cones, Hussaini Bridge, Gulmit village.
  • Day 4 — Adventure: Khunjerab Pass day trip (Pakistan–China border, 4,693 m) or a valley hike.
  • Day 5 — Departure: breakfast, last bazaar shopping, return to Gilgit.

🌆 Dubai — Five Days of Skyline & Sand

Skyscrapers, souks and desert sunsets. Plan the journey, enjoy the moment.

5 days / 4 nights Leisure & Exploration Friends / Family / Couple Budget: $1,000 per person (estimated, on-ground, excluding international flights)

Highlights

  • Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall & Fountain
  • Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina & JBR
  • Old Dubai souks and abra ride across the Creek
  • Desert Safari, Dubai Frame & Museum of the Future
  • Miracle Garden (seasonal) & Kite Beach

The five days

  • Day 1 — Arrival: check in, evening walk at Dubai Marina and JBR.
  • Day 2 — Discover: Burj Khalifa in the morning, Dubai Mall and aquarium, Fountain show in the evening.
  • Day 3 — Culture: Al Fahidi neighbourhood, abra ride, Gold Souk and Spice Souk.
  • Day 4 — Adventure: desert safari — dune bashing, camel ride, sunset photos, BBQ dinner.
  • Day 5 — Departure: Dubai Frame or Miracle Garden, last shopping, fly out.

🕌 Istanbul — Five Days Where Two Continents Meet

Palaces, bazaars and the Bosphorus. Plan the journey, enjoy the moment.

5 days / 4 nights Leisure & Exploration Friends / Family / Couple Budget: $800 per person (estimated, on-ground, excluding international flights)

Highlights

  • Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque & Topkapi Palace
  • Basilica Cistern & Süleymaniye Mosque
  • Grand Bazaar & Spice Bazaar
  • Galata Tower, Istiklal Street & Bosphorus cruise
  • Dolmabahçe Palace & Princes' Islands

The five days

  • Day 1 — Arrival: check in around Sultanahmet, gentle evening walk, dinner near the mosques.
  • Day 2 — Discover: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern, Topkapi Palace.
  • Day 3 — Culture: Grand Bazaar, Spice Bazaar and Eminönü street food, Süleymaniye Mosque.
  • Day 4 — Adventure: Bosphorus cruise between Europe and Asia, Galata Tower, Istiklal Street.
  • Day 5 — Departure: Dolmabahçe Palace or Çamlıca Hill, last bazaar shopping, fly out.
