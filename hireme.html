Terms & Conditions

The same terms for everyone — job seeker and employer alike. By signing up, you agree to all of them. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

1. What HIRE ME is

HIRE ME is a free website that connects job seekers and employers. It is only a source and a link between the two sides. HIRE ME is not an employer, not a recruitment agency, and not a party to any job. The job, the pay, the working hours and the work itself are agreed between the seeker and the employer — not by this website.

2. Free — no fees, ever

Signing up, uploading a CV, posting a job, applying and messaging are free. No fees, no commission, now or later. Nobody on HIRE ME should ever ask you for money to apply, to be hired, or to get a job. If someone does, do not pay — report them.

3. For job seekers

Give true information about yourself, your skills and your experience. Your profile and CV are visible to employers on this site so they can find you. You are responsible for checking an employer and a job offer before you accept anything. HIRE ME does not guarantee you a job, an interview, or any income.

4. For employers

Post only real jobs you genuinely intend to fill, with true details about the work, the pay and the place of work. You are responsible for checking a candidate before you hire. HIRE ME does not guarantee you a suitable candidate.

5. The perfect match

HIRE ME helps both sides find each other — search, skills, profiles and messages — so the right person and the right job can meet. But the match is yours to judge. Talk, ask questions, and agree everything — the work, the pay and the start date — before you start.

6. Stay safe

Never pay to apply or to be hired. Never share bank details, passwords, or photos of your ID with someone you met here before you have verified them. If a job, a message or a person feels wrong, stop and report it.

7. Your data

Your name, profile and CV are shown to other signed-in users so employers can find you and seekers can be found. Do not upload anything you do not want another user to see. To have your profile removed, email leaderoftime786@gmail.com.

8. Accounts

One account per person. Keep your password to yourself. Do not post fake jobs, fake CVs, or anything unlawful. If you break these terms, your account can be removed without notice.

9. Changes

These terms can be updated. The version on this page is the one that applies.
